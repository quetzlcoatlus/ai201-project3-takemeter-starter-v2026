# Category Ideas

**Suggestion = Sincerely proposes something concrete the reader could do, whether phrased as an instruction ("you should…"), as the writer's own experience ("I've found that hosting helps…"), or as a wish for something specific the OP could make ("I want to see a Scyther scythe").**
```You should play platinum and either heart gold or soul silver```

```Sounds like you need to start playing some rom hacks. They are usually based off Gen III games so you’ll feel right at home while getting to experience some new stories / Pokémon```

**Explanation = States a fact or explains how or why something works (game mechanics, lore, data, how something was made), where the point is what's true rather than what the writer prefers.**
```It should be noted that there is no Switch game that Spinda can come from and go into Home. Spinda is in BDSP, but because of a coding error, it can not go to Home. You can't even bring one from Pokemon Go because those are set patterns and as such, can not be transferred to Home.```

```Officially, none of them get plural forms```

**Opinion = Takes a position on Pokémon in general (species, types, games, designs, official content, franchise decisions), including a favourite, a prediction, or a self-labelled headcanon, even when it's phrased as a joke.**
```My favourite has always been and will always be Jynx. Totally dig Smoochum too. I know they have fans but … most people hate them and it’s unfortunate.```

```Poison all the way, baby. I've never had as much fun playing competitive as I have with my monotype poison team. And there are just so many cool mons. Second is definitely ground.```

**Reaction = Responds to the post with a feeling, joke, genuine question, praise or criticism of fan-made content, a pleasantry, or a personal moment, memory or result, without explaining, advising, or taking a position on Pokémon in general.**
```Such an elegant depiction of the pokemon. Lovely art!```

```Zangoose -> Zangeese```

## Decision Checklist

Apply in order and stop at the first step that matches. The Boundaries and Mixed Comment Rule below explain each step.

1. Spam, self-promotion, bot reply, or deleted? → **drop** (Exclusion rule)
2. Contains a sincere, standalone recommendation of an action the reader could take? → **suggestion** (Boundaries 3, 6, 7, 11; Mixed rule 1)
3. States facts or explains how something works? → **explanation**, unless the facts are there to justify a judgement → **opinion** (Boundaries 1, 8, 10; Mixed rule 1)
4. Takes a position on Pokémon in general that's visible in the text? → **opinion**. If it also reacts to the post, the takeaway decides between **opinion** and **reaction** (Boundaries 2, 5, 7, 9, 10; Mixed rule 2)
5. Anything else → **reaction**

## Boundaries

1. **Explanation vs opinion.** If the takeaway is a judgement or preference, it's opinion, even with facts in it. It's explanation only if the takeaway is how something works or what's true. Explanation wins only when it's a separate point.
2. **Opinion vs reaction.** Judging fan-made content in the post (art, crafts, animations, music) or your own result ("I got Ghost, not bad") is reaction. Judging official content (games, anime, movies, trailers, announcements) is opinion, even when the post is showing it. If the text names what's being judged but you can't tell whether it's fan-made or official ("Mega Banette is a bit too strong"), treat it as official: opinion. Otherwise, opinion needs a claim about Pokémon beyond the post.
3. **Joke advice is reaction.** If a suggestion obviously isn't meant to be followed ("Step 1: buy every game"), it's reaction. The joke has to be in the content (absurd or impossible advice). Laughter markers like "lol" or "😂" don't make sincere advice a joke.
4. **An enthusiastic opener doesn't disqualify** explanation or suggestion ("Really cool! It's because Foresight…").
5. **Label what the text shows.** If the label depends on context the comment doesn't show, label from the text alone. If the text doesn't show what's being judged ("Same, the second one is way better"), it's reaction.
6. **Suggestion vs explanation.** It's suggestion if it recommends an action ("you should", "try", "use", "you need to", or an imperative) or says an action helps ("I've found that hosting helps"). It's explanation if it only says what's possible or true ("you can get Ditto at…", "it's found in…"). Standard rules or procedures ("the Nuzlocke rules are…", "eat a sandwich, then check the basket") are explanation even when written as imperatives. Choosing a strategy or giving a tip ("spread it to as many mons as you can") is suggestion.
7. **Suggestion vs opinion.** A recommendation the reader can't act on, like a wish aimed at Game Freak ("GF should bring back the Battle Frontier"), is opinion even though it uses "should". A wish for something specific the reader could make or do ("I want to see a Scyther scythe" on a weapon-art post) is suggestion, because the reader can act on it. Asking whether something already exists isn't a wish ("let me know if you've done any Flygon art"), so it stays reaction. General encouragement ("keep it up, I'd love to see more") is a pleasantry (Boundary 11). Judging a game as good for beginners without recommending an action ("Let's Go is perfect for beginners") is opinion, because reading it as a recommendation needs the thread's context (Boundary 5).
8. **Hedges don't change the label.** Phrases like "I think", "pretty sure" or "IIRC" don't change the label, because nothing here is verified and every comment is read in good faith. A hedged fact ("I think Spinda can't transfer because of a coding error") is still explanation. Only a question genuinely asking for information is reaction. Proposals ("Have you considered…", "How about…") and rhetorical questions ("a bit too strong, isn't it?") take the label of their content. If a question comes with a hedged claim that passes the canon test (Boundary 10), the claim decides: "Wait, doesn't Foresight only remove the Ghost immunity? I thought Wonder Guard would still block the fighting moves" is explanation.
9. **Memories of a wrong belief are reaction.** "I always thought Phione evolved into Manaphy" is a personal memory, even if it adds what the writer thinks now.
10. **Speculation.** Guesses about how things are (lore, mechanics, design inspiration) are explanation (Boundary 8). That includes guesses about how an announced but unreleased mechanic works ("it might make you neutral to every type"). Predictions about what will be released or announced, and self-labelled headcanon ("my headcanon", "canon to me", "I like to think"), are opinion. Test for a guess: could it be checked against the games or canon, even in principle? If yes, it's explanation ("most Mystery Dungeon Pokémon eat berries?"). If it can only be agreed or disagreed with, it's opinion ("Lightning American was just early installment weirdness").
11. **Pleasantries aren't suggestions.** "Have fun", "enjoy" and "happy gaming" aren't concrete, so on their own they're reaction. Advice about how to play ("take your time", "just wing it") is still suggestion.

## Mixed Comment Rule

1. If a comment contains a complete, standalone suggestion or explanation (a full clause, not a fragment), that label wins, with suggestion > explanation. An explanation doesn't win if its facts are there to justify a judgement (Boundary 1).
2. Otherwise, between opinion and reaction, the takeaway decides. If the point of the comment is a position on Pokémon in general, it's opinion, even if most of the words are a personal result ("I just caught 5 shiny Applin, so I'm all for a new evolution"). If a comment reacts to fan-made content and also takes an explicit position on an official or franchise decision, the franchise position wins: opinion ("That's an amazing design! It's a shame Game Freak doesn't accept fan-made designs…").

## Exclusion rule

Drop spam, self-promotion, bot replies and deleted comments before labeling. Text copied word for word from an official announcement is fine to keep.
