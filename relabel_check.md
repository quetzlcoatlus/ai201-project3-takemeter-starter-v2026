```It's to avoid Pokemon having moves that aren't in the games they're in.

In SwSh, you can have Pokemon originated from Bank/Let's Go with moves that aren't in SwSh. While in-game, you can see these moves, they are not selectable, even having the description changed saying it should be forgotten.

I figure that, because of this, the easiest change while also future-proofing (noting that Arceus was developed alongside BDSP and that game has wildly different movesets for every Pokemon) would be to make Pokemon moved from HOME to BDSP-onward have their level up moveset for the current level they're in. However, if a Pokemon has previously been to the target game, its moveset will be whatever it was when it last left that game.

For example: If your Flareon originally was from BDSP and the only moves it learns are whatever the last four moves it naturally learns at the level it's in, if you move it to SwSh or SV, the default moveset it'll have will be exactly the same, since there are no notable changes between the three. However, if you move it instead to Arceus or Z-A, there will be changes because the level up moveset is different. Likewise, if ANY of Flareon's current moves are non-level up moves, like Sunny Day, Flame Charge, and Flamethrower, those get stored and replaced by its level up moves. You can still relearn Sunny Day and Flamethrower with Change Moves. For Flame Charge, though, because Flareon cannot learn it by level up, and Flame Charge isn't a TM in any Gen 8 game, it can't be learned (tbh I'm not sure myself if it can't relearn it in HOME when sent to SwSh/BDSP. ## 20
Label: Suggestion
```A few of the big notes.

Speed is king. Crit chance is determined by speed, so a fast pokemon using a high crit-chance move will crit an absurd amount of the time. Persian with Slash can be a monster.

Some things are just broken. Using focus energy actually lowers your crit chance instead of raising it. Look online for similar bugs.

Badge boost. When you receive a badge, it increases your stats, right? Well, when you use a stat-boosting move, it also accidentally reapplies the badge boost. So if you use Agility, you're not just raising your speed; you're raising everything at once. It also applies when your stats are lowered: of an enemy uses String Shot to lower your speed, it's actually increasing your other stats when it does that.

Binding moves like Wrap and Bind prevent the opponent from attacking. So if Brock catches you in a Bind, you could be stuck waiting for 5 turns before you can do anything about it.```
